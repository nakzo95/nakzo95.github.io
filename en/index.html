Why LinSol?

Everything in one install

Modeling, drawings, rendering, analysis and CAM are not separate products or paid add-ons. They all work in the same window, on the same file.

No internet required

No account and no sign-in each time you start it. Your files stay on your computer.

Built for Turkey, ready in English

Full English and Turkish interfaces. The TBDY 2018 seismic code and the RAL color chart are built in.

Open file formats

STEP files keep part names and colors, DXF files carry real dimension entities. Your data opens in other programs too.

Light and fast

About a 400 MB install. Real-time viewing and rendering run smoothly on an ordinary gaming graphics card.

Always up to date

The program tells you when a new version is out, and updates arrive as signed packages.

Tester programme

Test it,
use it for life.

Try LinSol on your own work and send a short report every month. In return your licence is free for life.

  • Your application goes to the developer; if accepted, your licence key is sent to your email address.
  • Every month you fill in a short feedback report inside the program: what you used, where you got stuck, what is missing.
  • In the last week of the month the program reminds you. If the report has not been sent when the month ends, LinSol will not open until it is sent.
  • As long as you send your reports your licence does not expire, and there is no charge.

Your details are kept in a private record store and are not public; they are used only to assess your application and to send your licence key.
